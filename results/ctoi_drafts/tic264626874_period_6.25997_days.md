## Notes (proposed text for the CTOI notes field)

Found in a BLS search of QLP Sector 98 FFI light curves. Recovered in the Sector 32 TESS-SPOC light curve (SNR 45.9).
Passes the LEO-Vetter flux-level tests. Difference images place the transit on the target in Sectors 32 and 98
(joint SNR 13.4). Odd-even depth difference 1.3 sigma; no secondary eclipse (-18 +/- 34 ppm). Fitted stellar density
0.97 of the TIC value. TRICERATOPS FPP 0.17 +/- 0.13, NFPP 0 (5 runs); earlier runs gave 0.015 and 0.075.
Host: LAMOST A1V, Teff 9600 K (TIC), Gaia vbroad 185 km/s, RUWE 1.19. Ellipsoidal variation at the transit period
163 +/- 107 ppm (1.5 sigma); a 0.1 solar mass companion gives about 160 ppm, so a low-mass star or brown dwarf is not
excluded. Suitable for Doppler tomography or RV follow-up.
