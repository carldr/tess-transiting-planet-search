## Notes (proposed text for the CTOI notes field)

Ultra-short-period Neptune-size candidate around an F star. Found in a BLS search of QLP Sector 101 FFI light
curves. Recovered in Sectors 10 (TESS-SPOC, SNR 7.5), 64 (QLP, 7.7), 99 (QLP, 11.4) and 100 (QLP, 11.2); joint SNR
18.3 against 5.5 for the highest null trial. Passes the LEO-Vetter flux-level tests. Joint difference image of all
sectors places the transit on the target (SNR 6.3). Odd-even 0.85 sigma; no secondary eclipse (18 +/- 23 ppm).
Fitted stellar density 1.2 times the TIC value. TRICERATOPS FPP 0.010 +/- 0.001, NFPP 1e-8 (5 runs). Caution:
Gaia DR3 RUWE 3.01 and RV variable (38 epochs, scatter 10.3 km/s), which points to a close stellar companion; its
light would dilute the transit, so the true radius can be larger. High-resolution imaging needed.
