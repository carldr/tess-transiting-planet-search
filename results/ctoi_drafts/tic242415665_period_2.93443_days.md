## Notes (proposed text for the CTOI notes field)

Found in a BLS search of QLP Sector 101 FFI light curves, and again in Sector 102. Recovered in Sectors 11
(TESS-SPOC, SNR 12.6), 38 (TESS-SPOC, 16.4), 65 (QLP, 12.7) and 102 (QLP, 13.3); joint SNR 29.7 against 7.1 for the
highest null trial. Passes the LEO-Vetter flux-level tests. Joint difference image places the transit on the target
(SNR 8.0). Odd-even 2.98 sigma, just below the 3 sigma limit; no secondary eclipse (32 +/- 47 ppm). The fitted
stellar density is 6.7 times the TIC value: the star may be smaller than the TIC gives (R* 1.47, logg 4.13), which
would make the companion smaller. TRICERATOPS FPP 0.47 +/- 0.03, NFPP 5e-4 (5 runs): not statistically favoured
over an eclipsing binary. Gaia DR3 RUWE 0.94, RV not variable (9 epochs).
