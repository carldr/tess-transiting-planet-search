## Notes (proposed text for the CTOI notes field)

Found in a BLS search of QLP Sector 103 FFI light curves. Recovered in the TESS-SPOC light curves of Sectors 12
(SNR 23.5) and 39 (SNR 28.4); joint SNR 46.9. Passes the LEO-Vetter flux-level tests. Difference image places the
transit on the target in Sector 103 (SNR 16.1); Sectors 12 and 39 are too faint to locate it. Odd-even 0.45 sigma;
no secondary eclipse (84 +/- 131 ppm). TRICERATOPS FPP 0.11 +/- 0.02, NFPP 0 (5 runs). Gaia DR3 RUWE 0.98, but RV
variable (14 epochs, chi-square p 0, scatter 5.5 km/s): a companion on the transit orbit is below 0.051 solar masses,
so the RV variation needs a brown dwarf or another body. The fitted stellar density is 5.0 times the TIC value (TIC
R* 1.39, logg 4.05); a smaller star gives a smaller companion. TIC contamination ratio 0.58; a G 14.3 star 14 arcsec
away could cause the dip with a 4% eclipse. RV and high-resolution imaging needed.
