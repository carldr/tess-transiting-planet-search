## Notes (proposed text for the CTOI notes field)

Found in a BLS search of QLP Sector 101 FFI light curves. Recovered in Sectors 11 (TESS-SPOC, SNR 8.6), 38
(TESS-SPOC, 7.9), 64 (TESS-SPOC, 9.2), 65 (TESS-SPOC, 9.8), 99 (QLP, 8.9) and 100 (QLP, 7.6); joint SNR 20.5 against
6.5 for the highest null trial. Passes the LEO-Vetter flux-level tests. Joint difference image places the transit on
the target (SNR 9.1). Odd-even 1.26 sigma; no secondary eclipse (14 +/- 22 ppm). The fitted stellar density is 14
times the TIC value: the TIC gives a subgiant (R* 3.55, logg 3.40), but the transit fits a smaller star, which would
make the companion about 3 to 4 Earth radii. TRICERATOPS FPP 0.030 +/- 0.008, NFPP 9e-24 (5 runs). Gaia DR3 RUWE
0.82; RV variable (16 epochs, scatter 3.0 km/s). TIC contamination ratio 0.27.
