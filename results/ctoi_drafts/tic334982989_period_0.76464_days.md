## Notes (proposed text for the CTOI notes field)

Ultra-short-period sub-Neptune candidate. Found in a BLS search of QLP Sector 101 FFI light curves. Recovered in
Sectors 10 (TESS-SPOC, SNR 9.3), 64 (QLP, 7.5), 99 (QLP, 9.2) and 100 (QLP, 11.0); joint SNR 18.4 against 6.4 for
the highest null trial. Passes the LEO-Vetter flux-level tests. Difference images place the transit on the target

between sectors. Fitted stellar density 1.6 times the TIC value. TRICERATOPS FPP 0.017 +/- 0.001, NFPP 9e-6 (5
runs). Host: TIC Teff 5511 K, R* 0.97, 214 pc; Gaia DR3 RUWE 0.99, RV not variable (27 epochs).
